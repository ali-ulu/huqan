## 1. Parties and purpose

This Individual Contributor License Agreement (the “Agreement”) governs copyright and patent rights in contributions that an individual intentionally submits to the HUQAN project (the “Project”) for possible inclusion.

The Project Owner provided for this review draft is **Ali Ulu**. The review notice email is `aliulu@ai-ulu.com`. The Project is the repository [`ali-ulu/huqan`](https://github.com/ali-ulu/huqan).

Before adoption, the Project Owner must confirm whether the rights are received personally or through a legal entity, whether the notice email is sufficient, and whether the proposed governing law and dispute provisions are appropriate. No commercial rights are granted by this review draft.

## 2. Definitions

“**Contribution**” means any original work of authorship or invention that You intentionally submit to the Project for inclusion, including source code, documentation, configuration, tests, examples, design material, patches, issue attachments, or other copyrightable or patentable material submitted through a project-approved channel.

“**You**” or “**Your**” means the individual accepting this Agreement.

“**Project Owner**” means the person or legal entity authorized to receive the rights described in this Agreement.

“**Submit**” means transmitting a Contribution to the Project through a pull request, issue, patch, email, review attachment, or another channel expressly accepted by the Project maintainers.

“**Project License**” means the license identified in the Project’s current `LICENSE` and package metadata. At the time of this review draft, the Project License is `AGPL-3.0-only`.

## 3. Scope and submission condition

This Agreement applies only to Contributions that You intentionally Submit after accepting the operative version of this Agreement through the Project’s approved acceptance process. It does not require You to Submit any Contribution and does not apply to unrelated work that You do not Submit to the Project.

A maintainer may reject, remove, or decline to distribute a Contribution. Acceptance of a Contribution into a pull request, branch, release, or repository does not by itself prove that the Contribution satisfies this Agreement or that the Project Owner has accepted any restriction that You did not disclose.

## 4. Contributor representations

You represent that, to the best of Your knowledge:

1. You are legally entitled to Submit the Contribution and to grant the rights described in this Agreement.
2. The Contribution is Your original work, or You have obtained sufficient written permission to Submit it and grant these rights.
3. No employer, client, school, government body, contractor, or other organization owns or controls rights in the Contribution unless You have obtained the required authorization or disclosed the restriction before acceptance.
4. The Contribution does not knowingly violate another person’s or organization’s copyright, patent, trade-secret, confidentiality, privacy, publicity, or other rights.
5. You have identified material third-party code, data, documentation, generated material, or other content whose license or provenance may limit the rights available to the Project.
6. You are not Submitting confidential information, credentials, personal data, export-controlled material, or other information that You are not authorized to disclose.
7. You will promptly notify the Project Owner if You discover a material error in any representation or a restriction affecting the Contribution.

These representations do not require You to guarantee that the Contribution is error-free or that no person could ever assert a claim. They require You to Submit only material that You are authorized to contribute and to disclose known restrictions.

## 5. Copyright license grant

Subject to this Agreement, You grant the Project Owner and recipients of the Project a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free, irrevocable copyright license to reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly display, publicly perform, sublicense, and distribute the Contribution and derivative works of the Contribution.

The grant is intended to permit the Project Owner to distribute the Contribution under the Project License, including `AGPL-3.0-only`, and, where the Project Owner has the legal authority and elects to do so, under a separate commercial license or other licensing terms. The Project Owner may also authorize Project recipients to exercise the rights necessary to use and distribute the Contribution under the selected Project License.

You retain ownership of Your copyright in the Contribution unless You and the Project Owner execute a separate written assignment. This Agreement does not transfer ownership of unrelated work, trademarks, patents, or personal data.

## 6. Patent license

To the extent that Your Contribution is covered by patent claims that You can license, You grant the Project Owner and recipients of the Project a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free patent license to make, have made, use, offer to sell, sell, import, and otherwise transfer the Contribution and derivative works of the Contribution, solely as necessary to exercise the copyright rights granted in this Agreement or to use the Contribution as part of the Project.

If You or an entity controlled by You initiates patent litigation alleging that the Project, the Contribution, or the combination of the Contribution with the Project infringes a patent, the patent license for the asserted Contribution may terminate prospectively as to the asserting party, subject to applicable law and qualified legal review. This defensive-termination language, its scope, and its treatment of affiliates and downstream recipients must be confirmed before adoption.

## 7. Moral rights, attribution, and publicity

To the extent permitted by applicable law, You waive or agree not to assert moral rights and similar rights in the Contribution to the extent necessary for the Project Owner and recipients to exercise the licenses granted above. Where waiver is not permitted, You grant the permissions necessary for those rights not to prevent the licensed uses.

This Agreement does not grant the Project Owner permission to use Your name, image, biography, employer identity, or public endorsement for marketing. Attribution, copyright notices, and third-party notices must be handled consistently with the Project License and applicable law.

## 8. Third-party, employer, and AI-assisted material

You must not Submit copied code, employer-owned work, client-owned work, confidential material, or a dependency with incompatible terms unless the relevant permission and license information has been disclosed and approved through the Project’s contribution process.

AI-assisted material is not automatically excluded. You remain responsible for reviewing the Contribution, checking its provenance to the extent reasonably possible, identifying material generated or adapted content, and confirming that You have the right to Submit it. AI assistance does not by itself transfer ownership or authorization to the Project Owner.

The Project Owner may request a provenance note, dependency notice, employer authorization, or other evidence before accepting a Contribution. No private evidence should be placed in the public repository.

## 9. No obligation and no warranty

The Project Owner is not required to accept, merge, publish, maintain, support, or retain any Contribution. Unless separately agreed in writing, Contributions are provided on an “AS IS” basis without warranties, representations, or conditions of any kind, to the maximum extent permitted by applicable law.

This Agreement does not create employment, agency, partnership, fiduciary, support, maintenance, service-level, or payment obligations between You and the Project Owner.

## 10. Acceptance, identity, and records

The operative Agreement must be accepted through a documented process selected by the Project Owner, such as an approved electronic signature record or an approved CLA service. A pull-request checkbox or a statement in `CONTRIBUTING.md` is not, by itself, a substitute for an operative signed Agreement unless qualified counsel confirms that the exact process is sufficient.

The acceptance record should identify only what is reasonably necessary:

| Record field | Required decision before adoption |
|---|---|
| Contributor legal name | Confirm collection and verification method |
| GitHub username or project identity | Confirm identity matching method |
| Email | Confirm whether required and how it is protected |
| Employer or contracting entity | Collect only when relevant to rights ownership |
| Agreement version | Record the exact version accepted |
| Acceptance date and time | Record the timestamp and applicable time zone |
| Covered Contribution or pull request | Link the acceptance to the relevant submission |

Acceptance records, emails, employer details, and other personal data must be stored privately, access-controlled, and retained only for a documented period. No contributor email, signature record, employer information, token, or unnecessary personal data should be committed to the public repository.

## 11. Corporate and representative contributions

An individual must not accept this ICLA on behalf of an employer, client, company, or other organization unless that authority is confirmed. A Contribution made in the course of employment, contract work, or commissioned work may require a separate corporate contributor agreement, written employer authorization, or assignment.

The Project Owner must define whether corporate contributions are accepted under a separate CCLA and how an individual’s acceptance interacts with that CCLA. Until that policy is approved, maintainers should request written clarification before merging a material corporate contribution.

## 12. Changes, versioning, and prior Contributions

The operative Agreement must carry a version identifier and effective date. A later version must not silently alter rights associated with an already accepted Agreement. Any change to rights already granted requires a separate legal decision and, where necessary, a new written agreement.

This review draft does not request a retroactive signature for the existing repository history. The Project Owner’s current maintainer decision is that no retroactive CLA request is planned for the existing work; that decision is not an independent legal ownership opinion.

The rights granted for an accepted Contribution survive rejection, removal, or termination of the contributor relationship, subject to applicable law and the final operative Agreement. This Agreement does not require future Contributions.
