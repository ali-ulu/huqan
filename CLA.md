## 2. Your representations

You represent that:

1. You are legally entitled to submit the Contribution.
2. The Contribution is either your original work or you have sufficient written permission to submit it under this Agreement.
3. To the best of your knowledge, the Contribution does not knowingly violate another person’s or organization’s copyright, patent, trade-secret, confidentiality, or other rights.
4. If an employer, client, school, or another organization may own or control rights in the Contribution, you have obtained the authorization required to submit it, or you will identify the relevant restriction before submission.
5. You will identify material third-party code, generated material, or other content whose license or provenance may limit the rights granted below.
6. You are not submitting confidential information, credentials, personal data, or material that you are not authorized to disclose.

These representations do not require you to guarantee that a Contribution is error-free or that no person could ever assert a claim. They require you to submit only material you are authorized to contribute and to disclose known restrictions.

## 6. Third-party and employer materials

You must not submit code copied from another project, an employer-owned work, a client-owned work, or a dependency with incompatible terms unless the relevant permission and license information has been disclosed and approved through the Project’s contribution process.

A contribution made with the assistance of an AI system is not automatically excluded. You remain responsible for reviewing the Contribution, checking its provenance to the extent reasonably possible, and confirming that You have the right to submit it. AI assistance does not transfer ownership or authorization to the Project Owner by itself.

## 7. Acceptance and records

The final Agreement must be accepted through a documented process selected by the Project Owner, such as a maintained electronic signature record or an approved CLA service. A pull-request checkbox or a statement in `CONTRIBUTING.md` is not, by itself, a substitute for an operative signed Agreement unless qualified counsel confirms that process is sufficient.

The Project should retain the accepted Agreement version, contributor identity, signature or acceptance record, date, employer/entity information where relevant, and the Contribution or pull request covered by the acceptance. Sensitive personal data should be stored privately and only for as long as necessary.

## 8. Changes to this draft

This draft may be revised before adoption. The operative version must be published with a version identifier and effective date. A later version must not silently alter the rights associated with an already accepted Agreement; any change to already granted rights requires a separate legal decision and, where necessary, a new written agreement.

## 9. Contributor information

The final signature or acceptance form should collect only the information required to identify the contributor and evidence authorization:

- Full legal name
- GitHub username or other project identity
- Email address for the private acceptance record
- Employer or contracting entity, if relevant
- Confirmation of authority to contribute
- Agreement version and acceptance date

Do not submit passwords, access tokens, payment information, or unnecessary personal data through the CLA process.
