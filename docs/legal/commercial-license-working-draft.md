## 2. Commercial option

HUQAN remains available under `AGPL-3.0-only`. A customer that requires a separate proprietary-use license may request a written commercial agreement for the specific HUQAN version and covered components identified in that agreement.

The commercial agreement is a separate license option. It does not retroactively replace the AGPL license for previously distributed versions and does not grant rights to components that the Project Owner is not legally authorized to relicense.

## 3. Proposed grant

Subject to payment, acceptance, and the final agreement, the Project Owner may grant the named customer a limited, non-transferable, non-sublicensable commercial license to use, reproduce, modify, compile, execute, and distribute the covered HUQAN components as part of the customer’s own proprietary product or internal service, within the number of products, deployments, users, or other limits stated in the agreement.

The exact grant must state whether it covers internal use, distribution to end users, hosted/network access, modifications, plugins, adapters, protocol implementations, documentation, examples, and future versions. No right should be implied beyond the written grant.

## 4. Exclusions and separate services

Unless expressly included in a separate signed agreement, the commercial license does not include:

- support, maintenance, response-time commitments, or an SLA;
- hosting, managed operations, deployment, or infrastructure;
- security certification, regulatory certification, or a guarantee of compliance;
- indemnity, warranty, or a promise that the software is suitable for a particular purpose;
- rights to third-party dependencies beyond the rights provided by their own licenses;
- rights to HUQAN trademarks, logos, or endorsements; or
- rights to future versions or separately identified modules.

## 5. Customer responsibilities

The customer remains responsible for its product, deployment, security controls, identity and access management, data protection, user notices, compliance obligations, and any changes it makes to HUQAN. HUQAN is a local-first trust and verification layer; it is not a replacement for the customer’s broader security or governance program.

The customer must not remove required third-party notices, misrepresent HUQAN’s capabilities, expose credentials or personal data through receipts or logs, or claim that HUQAN provides universal truth, universal connector enforcement, or third-party certification unless separately established in writing.

## 6. Third-party components

The final agreement must include a current third-party component and notice schedule. A commercial HUQAN license cannot grant rights that belong to an independent dependency owner. The customer must comply with the applicable licenses and notices for those components.

## 7. Term, fees, renewal, and termination

The final agreement must specify the license start date, term, renewal, fees, taxes, payment timing, permitted deployment scope, audit rights if any, and events that terminate the license. These fields are intentionally not fixed in this draft.

After termination, the agreement must state whether the customer may continue using already distributed copies, whether internal copies must be stopped or deleted, and which rights survive. This must be drafted consistently with the selected commercial model and applicable law.

## 8. Warranty, liability, confidentiality, and governing law

The final agreement must contain lawyer-reviewed provisions for warranties, disclaimers, limitation of liability, confidentiality, data processing where relevant, dispute resolution, governing law, notices, assignment, and the relationship between the commercial agreement and the AGPL-licensed public project. The proposed governing law is Türkiye, but no governing-law or dispute provision is operative in this draft.
